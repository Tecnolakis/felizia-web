Frequently asked questions

What people usually ask us

Have another question? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll get back to you.

Do I need to know about technology to work with you?

No. We take care of the technical side and explain everything in plain language. All we need is for you to tell us how your business works.

My business is small. Is AI useful for me?

Yes. Small businesses are exactly where it shows the most: automating just a few repetitive tasks frees up hours of work every week for what really matters.

How long does it take to see results?

We start with focused projects and clear goals, so you can see results in weeks, not months. Then we grow from what works.

What happens to my company's data?

We protect your information at all times: we define who can access what, use tools with sound security practices, and important decisions always go through someone on your team.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what you need. The first meeting is free of charge: we get to know your business and then send you a proposal with a clear scope and price, no surprises.

Do I have to change the systems I already use?

Not necessarily. Whenever possible, we integrate AI with the tools you already have, such as your management system, spreadsheets, email or WhatsApp.
